Identity lab

The wrong-key tripwire. Log in each account once, pick the identity you intend to sign as, and press sign. The page stamps that identity on the note the way a multi-account client does, then checks the signature that came back: green means it came from the account you meant, red means Sidecar signed with a different key and you should stop and look. Nothing publishes; these notes go nowhere.
