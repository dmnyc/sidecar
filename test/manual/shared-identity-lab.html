Shared-identity lab

Load Sidecar unpacked with two test accounts (not your main ones), and give them different names (say Alpha and Beta): the cards tell accounts apart by name and npub, and this page calls them A and B. Work down the page in order: each case starts from the state the one before it left. Each button sends a real request and logs what came back. “no card” means Sidecar answered in under a second, so it signed without asking; “card answered” means you answered a card. The ✓ or ✗ after a line checks only timing and signer; the rest you judge, then tick Pass or Fail. A red bar means a signature came from the wrong account.

This page was opened as a file, so it has no site name. Sidecar tells sites apart by name, so it refuses every request here at once (“Missing host”). Serve the page instead: run python3 -m http.server 8021 from the repo root, then open http://pick.localhost:8021/test/manual/shared-identity-lab.html.

  • The rule under test. On a site where both accounts are signed in, every card says “Confirm who’s posting each time.” and lets you pick the account from the capsule. A pick names the signer for that one signature. Nothing else moves: not Sidecar’s active account, not the account the site is pinned to. The next card opens as the account you came in with. The standing offers stay with that account too: a card showing a picked account carries no hint and no row, since a 30-minute window is something an account earns, and a one-off pick has earned none. Logging in is the one exception, where a pick is how the site learns who you are.
  • Sections. 0: sign in with both. 1: the pick in the side panel. 2: the same in the popup window. 3: the row and the hint. 4: Forget site. 5: the login exception. 6: each account’s permission level.
  • Reading a card. The quiet hint is two lines under the buttons: “Keep signing as …?” then “Open a 30-minute window.” It shows on the second and third ask of a kind. The row sits under the preview, “You've signed … 3 times in a row.”, with Sign for 30 min and Not now. It replaces the hint from the fourth ask. This page’s host has two accounts, so the row never offers Always sign.
  • Where to look. “Sidecar’s active account” is the name in the side panel’s header. “Pinned to” is the account on this site’s row in Activity → Connected sites. A pick must leave both alone (except in section 5).
  • Resets. A kind’s count: Not now on its row, or reloading the extension at chrome://extensions. A window: the ✕ on the status bar. Everything else: Forget site on this host’s row, then log in again (0.1).
  • Your ticks. Pass/Fail and logs survive a refresh. Clear this host’s results wipes them and the A/B labels, and nothing in Sidecar.
