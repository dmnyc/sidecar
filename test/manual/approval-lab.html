Approval lab

Load Sidecar unpacked with two test accounts (not your main ones), open the side panel, and work down the page in order: each case starts from the state the one before it left. Each button sends a real request and logs what came back. “no card” means Sidecar answered in under a second, so it signed without asking; “card answered” means you answered a card. The ✓ or ✗ after a line checks only timing and signer; the rest you judge, then tick Pass or Fail.
